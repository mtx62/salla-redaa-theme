# UI/UX recommendations — 2026-11-01

Approved by the user and implemented on 2026-11-01. Preserve the accepted header/footer. The five items below record the original proposals; implementation and verification are in CHANGELOG.md under Product experience and reviews refresh.

1. Keep the quick-view close control visible while its body scrolls. In the reported product the current close control scrolls out of view with the image section. Preserve Escape, backdrop close and focus restoration.
2. On mobile, put the nine image thumbnails in one horizontally scrollable strip rather than wrapping them over multiple lines. This keeps title, price and required options closer to the first screen.
3. Present gifting as a compact secondary disclosure or row after purchase options. Keep the feature available while giving product selection and purchase stronger hierarchy.
4. Superseded by the user on 2026-11-01: omit the entire rating row when no actual star rating exists, including the no-reviews label. Keep real comments.
5. Put real category-specific attributes on cards (such as material/dimensions when provided), so users can decide which product deserves a quick view. Never fill missing attributes with invented data.

Reference: Baymard recommends sufficient product-list information rather than using quick views to compensate for weak cards: https://baymard.com/research-articles/product-listing-information
